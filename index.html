Fitness tips

The fundamentals that matter more than any single exercise.

01

Eat in a surplus

Aim for a slight calorie surplus of +300–500 kcal per day.

02

Train consistently

Train 4–5 days per week, and rest properly between sessions.

03

Progressive overload

Increase the weights every 1–2 weeks to keep muscles growing.

04

Stay hydrated

Drink around 3 litres of water every day.

05

Sleep 7–8 hours

Muscles grow during rest, so treat sleep as part of training.
